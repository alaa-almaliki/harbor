Harbor backlog
==============

- Pre-import backups omit stored routines and events.
  The auto-backup taken before `db import`/`db pull`/`db restore` dumps with
  `--single-transaction --no-tablespaces` and no `--routines`/`--events`, so
  stored procedures, functions, and scheduled events are NOT captured (triggers
  are, by mysqldump default). Restoring such a backup therefore loses them.
  Fix: add `--routines --events` (and keep `--triggers` explicit) to the
  pre-import dump in `_db_autobackup` (lib/db.sh). Trade-off: larger dumps.
  Its own change — behavior/size impact on every project's backups.
